THE TWO-PLANE CLAIMS ARCHITECTURE (H5 = H1-derivations + H4-scoped-cross-contract + facts hardening + registry governance).

WHAT A LABEL IS: a typed claim `{label, tier, witness}` about a function's effect, where the claim sentence is fixed in a code registry and the witness is a machine-replayable pointer into the facts plane or the trees. A label without a registered evidence spec cannot be constructed (schema-level, not convention).
